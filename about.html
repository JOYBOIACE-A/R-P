Who We Are

R&P Shipping & Logistics is a modern logistics and product sourcing company dedicated to providing customers with access to quality products from different parts of the world.

We specialize in connecting buyers with trusted suppliers while providing efficient shipping solutions, ensuring safety, reliability and convenience.

Our goal is to make international purchasing and logistics simple, affordable and stress-free.
